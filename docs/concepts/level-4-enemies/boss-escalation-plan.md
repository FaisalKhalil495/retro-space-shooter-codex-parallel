# Breakwater revision 3 — approved escalation plan

The owner requested removal of Breakwater's laser, a harder replacement, and
new attacks and tactics with every phase. They explicitly approved this plan.
This is a revision of the separate boss preview, not campaign Level 4.

Replace the boss beam with **Hunter Torpedoes**: small, shootable missiles
that chase briefly, then commit to a straight run. The player can shoot them
down or dodge after they commit.

| Phase | Approved direction |
| --- | --- |
| 1: 100–66% | Broadside, flak, skipping shells and two-torpedo salvos. Introduce the foundations clearly. |
| 2: 66–33% | Introduce Twin Darts and ice eruptions; three torpedoes. Respond to high flyers with darts and low flyers with ice, and press farther forward. |
| 3: below 33% | Introduce rush-with-flak and torpedoes-followed-by-ice sequences. Standalone salvos reach four missiles; combinations use fewer. |

After introductions, vary attack selection and limit repeats. Rest targets:
0.45 / 0.30 / 0.18 seconds. Open the cannon chamber during missile launches
and short recoveries. Warn each attack and verify a reachable escape route
through complete combined sequences using the original ship's movement.

Keep 250 HP, the 58×24 icebreaker design, the ten-part destruction, the original
hero and bullets, all player weapons (including the player's laser), original
controls, adaptive supplies, saving and earlier levels. New boss music and
campaign integration remain separate stages.

Review with real captures and a fresh portable preview. Compare evasive play
against the unchanged Glacier Warden, while acknowledging the limits of an
